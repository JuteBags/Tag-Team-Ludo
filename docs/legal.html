Terms of use

By using Tag Team Ludo you agree to these terms. They're short on purpose.

1. The game

Tag Team Ludo is a free game, provided by JuteBags ("we"). We may change, improve or remove features, including online play, at any time.

2. Fair play

  • Play honestly. Don't cheat, use bots or scripts in online games, exploit bugs, or attack or overload our servers.
  • Leaving an online game counts as a forfeit, as the rules in the app describe.
  • When no one is found in time, Ranked games up to Gold III may include computer players of a similar strength; they count like any game.
  • We may reset ratings, remove Gems or block a guest account that breaks these rules.

3. Your display name and chat

Choose a name that isn't offensive, hateful, or someone else's identity. In chat, be kind: no insults, hate, threats or personal details. We may change or remove names, and block a guest account, that break this rule.

4. Gold, Gems and skins

Gold and Gems are in-game items with no money value. They can't be bought with or exchanged for real money, and they can't be transferred. Local gold lives on your device; if you delete the app or its data, it's gone. Online Gems and ratings may be reset if online play changes.

The game is provided "as is". We do our best, but we can't promise it will always work, be available, or be free of bugs, and we're not liable for any loss from using it, as far as the law allows.

Privacy note

Tag Team Ludo is made to collect as little as possible. Here is everything we touch, in plain words.

Playing on this device (vs Computer, pass the phone, puzzles)

  • Everything stays on your device: your settings, gold, skins, achievements, past games, reviews, puzzle streaks and the names you type for friends. It's stored in the app's own storage (or your browser's, on the website). We never receive it.
  • Ads and analytics: to keep the game free, the game may show ads or use analytics services. When it does, this note will list what they collect and who runs them, and the app will ask you to accept the updated version.
  • The update check: the app asks our website whether a newer version exists (a plain file download). That request carries nothing about you; the hosting service (GitHub Pages) may keep its usual short-lived server logs, such as your IP address.
  • Sharing a game: a game is shared only when you tap Share. When online play is set up and you've accepted this document, the game (and its review) is kept on our game server and the link carries only a short code; otherwise the whole game rides in the link itself. Anyone with the link can see the game. We keep a shared game for a year after it was last opened.
  • Games you share, and online games, may be used to calibrate the accuracy and luck measures. A shared game shows bots' names and colours in place of people's names.
  • Clearing it: uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes everything.

Playing online (private rooms and Ranked)

Online play needs a small amount of data on our game server:

  • A guest account: a random ID made on your device, plus a secret that proves the account is yours (we keep only a scrambled version of it). No email, phone number, real name or password is asked for.
  • The display name you choose, shown to the other players in your games.
  • Your online stats: your rating and tier for each mode, and later your Gems.
  • The games you play online, while they're being played (the server rolls the dice and checks the moves), so a game can survive a dropped connection.
  • Connection data: our server runs on Cloudflare. Like any internet service, it sees your IP address while you're connected, and Cloudflare may keep short-lived logs for security.

We don't sell it. We use it to run online play: matching players, keeping ratings and keeping games fair.

  • Deleting your online data: ask us (see Contact) and we'll delete your guest account, name, ratings, friend code and friends list (and a game you shared, if you send us its link). Deleting the app alone doesn't remove the server copy.

Chat

In an online game you can send quick phrases, emoji and, in private rooms, short messages. Our server only passes each message on to the players in that game: it doesn't store chat. If you tap Report on a message, that one message, who sent it, who reported it and the room's code go to our server's log, so we can review it.

Friends

If you use Friends, our server keeps your friend code, the display name you use, and your friends list (the friends you added, and who added you) with your guest account, so your friends can see when you're online and invite you into a private room. Whether you're online is only known while the app is connected; it isn't stored. Removing a friend removes them from both lists.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone. Online play shows only a chosen display name and asks for no personal details. Free-text chat is only in private rooms, with a word filter, and anyone can be muted or reported. Please don't use your real full name as your display name, or share personal details in chat.
