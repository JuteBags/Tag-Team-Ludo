Why Tag Team Ludo

Same board and rules you grew up with, rebuilt for phones, with one move people play at home that most apps leave out.

Pair move

Two of your tokens on one square move together on an even roll. Only a pair can cut a pair.

Safe stars

Eight star squares where nobody can be captured. Run there when someone is right behind you.

Smart bots

Play 2, 3 or 4 player games against computer players that capture, escape and build blocks.

Web and Android

One game, two ways: right here in the browser, or as an Android app that plays fully offline.

How to play

Get all four of your tokens around the board and into the centre before the bots do.

Classic rules

  1. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base.
  2. A 6 gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row and you lose that roll.
  3. Land on a single enemy token on a normal square to send it home, and roll again.
  4. Stars are safe: tokens of any colour can share them.
  5. Walk your token round to your coloured lane and into the centre. You need the exact number to finish, and finishing a token gives another roll.
  6. First to bring all four tokens home wins.

The pair move (jodi)

  • Two of your tokens on the same square make a pair.
  • A pair moves on an even roll, half each: roll 4, both move 2.
  • On a normal square a pair is locked together: its tokens can't move alone. It splits again on a star or in your home lane.
  • On a star a pair is just two tokens: you choose to move both together or one on its own.
  • A single token can't jump an enemy pair. It can stop on it, though, and carry on from there next time.
  • Only a pair can cut a pair: land on one and both tokens go home. A pair can jump another pair, but it can't cut a single token.

Game design

A glossy 2.5D look drawn entirely in vector code: no image files on the board, so it stays sharp on any screen and the game stays small.

Board
A lacquered gold frame, raised tiles and four coloured bases. Turn order runs clockwise: Blue (you), Green, Red, Yellow.
Pieces and dice
Shaded pawns that hop square by square, and a real 3D die that tumbles on every roll.
Pairs you can see
A pair gets a ×2 badge; a gold ring underneath means it's blocking.
