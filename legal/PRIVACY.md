# Tag Team Ludo: Privacy note

Version 1 · 6 November 2026

Tag Team Ludo is made to collect as little as possible. Here is everything we touch, in plain words.

## Playing on this device (vs Computer, pass the phone, puzzles)
- **Everything stays on your device:** your settings, gold, skins, achievements, past games, reviews, puzzle streaks and the names you type for friends. It's stored in the app's own storage (or your browser's, on the website). We never receive it.
- **Ads and analytics:** to keep the game free, the game may show ads or use analytics services. When it does, this note will list what they collect and who runs them, and the app will ask you to accept the updated version.
- **The update check:** the app asks our website whether a newer version exists (a plain file download). That request carries nothing about you; the hosting service (GitHub Pages) may keep its usual short-lived server logs, such as your IP address.
- **Sharing a game:** a game is shared only when you tap Share. A shared game carries the moves and dice, and colours in place of people's names.
- **Clearing it:** uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes everything.

## Playing online (private rooms and Ranked)
Online play needs a small amount of data on our game server:
- **A guest account:** a random ID made on your device, plus a secret that proves the account is yours (we keep only a scrambled version of it). No email, phone number, real name or password is asked for.
- **The display name you choose,** shown to the other players in your games.
- **Your online stats:** your rating and tier for each mode, and later your Gems.
- **The games you play online,** while they're being played (the server rolls the dice and checks the moves), so a game can survive a dropped connection.
- **Connection data:** our server runs on Cloudflare. Like any internet service, it sees your IP address while you're connected, and Cloudflare may keep short-lived logs for security.

**We don't sell it.** We use it to run online play: matching players, keeping ratings and keeping games fair.

- **Deleting your online data:** ask us (see Contact) and we'll delete your guest account, name and ratings. Deleting the app alone doesn't remove the server copy.

## Children
The game is suitable for everyone. Online play shows only a chosen display name, has no chat, and asks for no personal details. Please don't use your real full name as your display name.

## Changes
If this note changes, the app will show it again and ask you to accept the new version.
