Tag Team Ludo 4.0: play online. Private rooms with your friends, Ranked games with a rating, and a friends list.

## Play with friends online
- **Private rooms:** create a room, share its 5-letter code, and your friends join from their own phones or computers. Pick the mode, doubles on or off, and how the computer plays the empty places.
- **Four modes:** 1v1v1v1, 1v1, two colours each, and 2v2 with a partner.
- **Chat in the room:** quick phrases, emoji, or type your own.
- **Rejoin:** lost the connection? Come back within a minute and carry on.
- **Fair play:** every roll comes from the server, and every move is checked.

## Ranked
- **A rating for every mode,** from Bronze up to Diamond, with points inside each division.
- **Matched with players near your rank.** Cancel any time while it searches.
- **Placement:** your first 10 games in a mode move your rating faster.
- **Quick chat** with phrases and emoji.
- Leaving a game counts as a forfeit.

## Friends
- **Add friends** with your friend code, see who's online, and invite them straight into your room.

## Reviews
- **Review my games automatically:** turn it off in Settings if you'd rather choose.
- **Pin up to 10 games** to keep their reviews.
- **A smoother progress bar** while a review runs.

## Settings and more
- **Privacy & Terms in one place,** asked once before you play online. Offline play works without it.
- **The colour-blind switch** now lives in Settings.
- **The Settings sheet scrolls properly** on small screens.
