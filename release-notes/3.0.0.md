Tag Team Ludo 3.0: a sharper game review that's up to 4× faster, Undo, double kills, and colours everyone can read.

## A sharper game review
- **Stricter, steadier labels.** Inaccuracy, Mistake and Blunder now start sooner. A move only gets the harsher label when it's clearly past the line, so a coin flip isn't called a mistake.
- **Turning points count.** A move that took you from winning to losing is marked one step harsher. A Best move is never marked down.
- **One unit everywhere:** every move and every roll shows what it was worth in "% to win".
- **Three 6s in a row** show as one moment, with what the cancelled turn cost.
- **Graphs:** Win %, Accuracy and the Luck-O-Meter, each on its own tab in the review. Your accuracy and luck over your last games are in Stats.
- **Say less:** the dice get a number, not a judgement. The headline is about how you played.
- **Colours for everyone:** the move labels use a colour-blind-safe set, and each keeps its own symbol (★ ✓ ?! ? ??).

## Much faster reviews
- **Reviews use more of your device:** up to 3 cores on a phone and up to 6 on a computer. That's about 2× faster on phones and up to 4× on computers, with exactly the same verdicts.
- **The first key move shows sooner,** while the rest of the game is still being checked.

## Play
- **Undo:** take back a move once or twice a game (the small x/2 button), until the next dice appear. It works in every mode.
- **Double kills:** capturing a pair gets a double finisher for every colour: two blades, two waves, a split bolt.
- **Green's new finisher:** the earth splits and the pawn falls in.
- **Fairer stopped games:** a game stopped early ranks players by tokens home first, then by how far along the board they are.

## Fixes
- **Clean games:** a game with no mistakes no longer gets a headline about mistakes.
- **Stats** count only finished reviews.
