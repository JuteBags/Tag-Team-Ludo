Tag Team Ludo 1.0: the first full version. New pair rules, a board that shows you the way, and rolls and kills that feel great.

## New pair (jodi) rules
- Land your token on your own token to make a **pair**. On a normal square a pair is **locked**: it moves only on an even roll, half each (4 → 2 + 2).
- On a **star** a pair splits: move both together, or one token alone.
- A single token can't jump an enemy pair, but it can **stop on it** and carry on past it later. A single sitting on an enemy pair can be cut by any other single.
- **Only a pair can cut a pair.** A pair can jump another pair, but it never cuts a single token.

## A clearer board
- Arrows at every corner where the track turns, U-turn arrows at the end of each arm, and `>>>` next to each start square.
- After you roll, every move you can make shows **where it lands**, with its steps and a tag for a kill, a safe star or home. Tap the token or the spot.
- A pair on a star shows "Pair" and "One" right on the board: no more pop-up.
- Tap a token that can't move and the game tells you why.
- Bigger pieces, a bold ring on the ones you can move, and easier tapping on phones.
- Badges show a locked pair (padlock) and a pair on a star (×2).

## Rolls and kills that feel great
- The die is shaken in a little cup and thrown onto the mat, and the camera zooms in on the result.
- A new rounded die with red 1 and 6. Choose whether the top face or the front face counts (pause menu).
- Kills hit hard: a big leap, a shake, sparks, and the cut token flies home. SIX!, KILL! and HOME! banners.
- Sounds and vibration, with a mute switch in the pause menu.
