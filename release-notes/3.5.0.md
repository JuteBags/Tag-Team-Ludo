Tag Team Ludo 3.5: your review is ready the moment the game ends, a daily puzzle, and a cleaner review screen.

## Reviews, instantly
- **Reviewed while you play:** each of your moves is checked in the background as the game goes on, so the review is ready the moment the game ends, even on a phone.
- **About 2× faster engine** on almost every phone and computer, with exactly the same verdicts. Older devices keep working at the old speed.
- **An honest countdown:** "time left" now matches what's really left.

## The new review screen
- **A clear switch** between your review and your opponents'.
- **Your accuracy and luck up front,** with tabs for Win %, Accuracy and the Luck-O-Meter.
- **Key moments** with the better move in one line, and a strip of every move in the game.

## Daily puzzle
- **One puzzle a day:** find the best move in a real position. Easier early in the week, harder at the weekend.
- **Expert:** an extra hard puzzle each day, for the ones even our Hard bot misses.
- **Streak and gold:** +20 gold for the best move, +5 for trying. Your puzzle streak is separate from the check-in.
- **The answer, explained:** your move against the better one on the board, why it's better, and one way the game could go from there.
- Find it next to the Store on the home screen.

## Settings
- **Dice sounds:** turn the dice rattle and landing off without muting everything else.
- **New players start with doubles off.** You can turn them on in Settings, and your saved choice is kept.

## Smoother
- **Calmer while you think:** the waiting animations settle after a few seconds, which saves battery.
- **A faster luck check** in the review.
