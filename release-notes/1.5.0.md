Tag Team Ludo 1.5: a palace to bring your tokens home to, smarter bots, and a game that keeps up with your life.

## The palace
- The centre of the board is now a **castle**, with a gate facing each colour's home lane.
- When one of your tokens gets home, a short cutscene welcomes it into your colour's kingdom: the **Ember Keep** (red), the **Emerald Grove** (green), the **Sun Citadel** (yellow) or the **Tide Palace** (blue), each with its own scenery, stick-figure locals and sounds. Tap to skip.
- New pawns: chunkier and glossier, and they sit right in the middle of their squares. Pairs are easier to read.

## Play your way
- **Bots at three levels:** Easy, Normal and Hard. Pick one in the game options; your last choice is remembered.
- **Keep playing?** When someone finishes, everyone still playing votes on whether to play on for the next place. One "stop" ends the game there.
- **Board themes:** Classic, Wooden and Dark, in Settings.
- **Colour-blind symbols:** ● green, ▲ red, ■ yellow, ◆ blue on every pawn and base. There's a switch right on the home screen.

## Never lose a game
- **Continue game:** your game is saved every turn. Closed the app, took a call, phone restarted? Pick up exactly where you left off.
- The screen stays on while you play, and the game pauses when you switch apps.
- The **back button** now pauses the game instead of closing the app.

## Share and track
- **Share your result** as a picture through WhatsApp or any app.
- **Your stats:** games, wins, win rate, best place, kills and pairs formed.
