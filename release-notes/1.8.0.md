Tag Team Ludo 1.8: roll your whole turn first, play classic Ludo without doubles, and set up a game with friends at a glance.

## Roll first, then play
- **Your whole turn is rolled first.** Every 6 rolls again, and the dice wait in a **tray** beside your dice. Then you play them in any order, on any token: tap a die, then a token.
- **Plan with everything you rolled.** Rolled 6, 6, 4? Use one 6 for the kill, then play the 6 and the 4 however you like.
- **A kill or a token home rolls again right away**, and the new dice join your tray.
- **Three 6s in a row** end the turn before anything moves.
- Dice no token can use are dimmed, and the bots show their tray too, so their turns are easy to follow.

## Classic Ludo, if you prefer
- **Settings → Doubles: Off** plays classic single-token Ludo: every token moves alone, nothing blocks, and landing on a square captures everything there.
- Doubles stay on by default, and a saved game keeps the rules it started with.

## With friends, made simple
- The game options are plain questions now: how many colours each player plays, where your two colours sit, whether to fill the empty colours with computer players, and how good the bots are.
- A small board preview shows who plays each house before you start.
