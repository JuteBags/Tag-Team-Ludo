Tag Team Ludo 2.0: a brand-new look, a Store full of skins, kill finishers, gold and achievements.

## A new look
- **The board leans back in 3D,** with raised tiles, thick bases, a deep frame and bigger, glossier pawns that stand above their squares. Games are played on a darker background, so the board stands out.
- **Kill finishers:** every kill is finished by the attacker's colour. Green blasts the victim back, Red brings down a guillotine and cuts it in two, Blue's wave sweeps it away, and Yellow's lightning burns it to ash. The camera zooms in on the hit. Choose Full, Short or Off in Settings.
- Clearer moves: a big **ROLL** button and your dice right by your thumb, pawns that can't move fade back, bigger landing markers, and one banner at a time, so **KILL!** is never covered up.

## The Store
- **Skins for pawns, dice, throwers and boards:**
  - **Pawns:** Skull, Ice-cream, Robot, Knight
  - **Dice:** Bone, Tron, Gold, Glass
  - **Throwers:** a skull that spits the die out, an ice-cream cone, the three-cups trick, a dice gun
  - **Boards:** Metal, Halloween, and **Tron**, with lines of light and light-cycle trails
- **Earn gold by playing** (more for winning, kills and tokens home) and spend it on skins.
- **Achievements:** First win, Untouchable, Giant slayer and more, each paying bonus gold. They're listed with your progress in Stats.

## Easier to play
- **Quick Start** vs Computer: no rolling or picking, you play Blue and go first.
- **Name your players,** remembered on your phone. After rolling for order, everyone slides into play order.
- **Pass the phone:** With friends, a card tells the next player it's their turn.
- **How to play** in the pause menu, a tip the first time your dice wait in the tray, and a **Fast bots** switch.
- Quitting or stopping a game now asks first, so nothing is lost by accident.
- Settings and Stats are clearer: proper switches, real mini-board previews for each board, win streaks and sixes rolled.

## Fixes
- Three 6s in a row now cancel only those three 6s. Dice you kept from before a kill stay in your tray.
- The palace scene fills tall screens, names no longer wrap, and the share card's footer no longer covers the last player.
- Smaller download and a smoother game on cheaper phones.
