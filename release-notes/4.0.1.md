Tag Team Ludo 4.0.1: friends front and centre, links you can tap, and the online screens redone.

## Friends
- **Friends on the home screen,** with a badge when friends are online or someone wants to add you.
- **Add friends after a game:** tap Add next to anyone you played with or against, your Teams partner too. They accept or decline.
- **Requests** wait in the Friends sheet, and pop up if you're playing when one arrives.

## Links you can tap
- **Share your room or your friend code as a link.** Tapping it opens the game and joins the room, or offers to send a friend request. On Android, it offers to open the app.
- **Share a game after the match:** your result card now comes with a short link to the whole game, its replay and review.
- **Copy codes with one tap,** in the app too.

## Pick a game at a glance
- **Four clear choices:** Free for all, Duel, Double duel and Teams, each with a small picture of who sits where.
- The same names everywhere: rooms, Ranked, the game's top bar and your rating.

## Rooms
- **A big room code** to copy or share straight away.
- **Seats you can read:** each player in their colour, and open seats marked once, with one line saying who fills them.
- **A wrong code** now says so and keeps what you typed, so you can fix it.
- **The room says clearly** when it's connecting or reconnecting.

## Ranked
- **Placement shows as a ring** filling up to 10 games, with no rank until you've earned one.
- **A cleaner search screen** with a big timer, and a full "Taking a while?" hint to play with friends instead.
- **Try again** if the server can't be reached.

## Fixes
- The friends row in the room menu had an oversized arrow.
- After an online game ends, the status bar no longer says "Sending…".
