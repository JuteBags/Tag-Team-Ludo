Tag Team Ludo 2.5: four bot levels up to a new Extreme, a review of every game, replays you can share, and gold for coming back.

## Bots worth beating
- **Four levels: Easy, Normal, Hard and Extreme,** each a clear step up from the one before. A line under the picker says how each one plays.
- **Extreme** looks ahead on every move. It's the strongest bot the game has had, in every mode: 2, 3 or 4 players, and two colours each.

## Review your game
- **Game review:** after a game, see how well you played. Every move you made gets a verdict (Best, Good, Inaccuracy, Mistake or Blunder), along with your accuracy and a graph of your winning chances.
- **Key moves first:** the moves that cost you the most come first, each with the better move. The rolls that swung the game are listed too, yours and your opponents'.
- **Dice luck in words:** see whether your dice were lucky, unlucky or average, so you know if it was you or the dice.
- Tap any move to watch it on the board. A review is kept with the game, so it opens instantly next time.

## Replays
- **Watch any of your last 20 games** again, move by move or playing by itself, from the result screen or from Stats.
- **Share a game as a link.** Whoever opens it watches the whole game in their browser. People's names stay on your phone; a shared game shows colours instead.

## Rewards
- **Daily check-in:** gold every day you play, growing over a 7-day streak.
- **New achievements** for beating each level and for check-in streaks.
- **Extreme streaks:** big gold for 3, 5 and 7 wins in a row against Extreme.
- **Two skins you can't buy:** Inferno dice for 5 Extreme wins in a row, and Crowned pawns for 7.

## Fairer games
- **A continued game rolls the dice it would have rolled,** so closing the app can't re-roll a bad turn.
- **Giving up against the Computer counts as a loss,** and the game tells you before you do.

## Easier to play
- **Choose your colour vs Computer:** tap a house in the preview. Quick Start remembers it.
- **The two-colours-each game against a bot** is now easy to find from 4 players.
- **Crowded moves get big numbered buttons,** so a choice is never hidden under a pawn or mistapped.
- **"Pass the phone"** shows once per person, before their first turn, not on every hand-over.
- **Update prompt:** the home screen tells you when a newer version is out.
- Lots of small polish: clearer achievement and gold totals on the result screen, an Achievements tab in Stats, and larger small text.
